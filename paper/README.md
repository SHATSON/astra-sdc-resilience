# Paper

`ASTRA_SDC_Journal_Manuscript.docx` is the manuscript, formatted for submission
to an Elsevier journal (target: *Journal of Parallel and Distributed Computing*):
single column, double spaced, continuous line numbers, numbered sections, and the
required CRediT, competing-interest, data-availability and acknowledgement
statements. References follow APA 7th edition author-date style, which Elsevier
accepts at submission provided it is applied consistently.

When opening it in Word, accept the prompt to update fields so that the table of
contents and the lists of figures and tables populate. Equations are native Word
equations and may appear blank in some non-Word previewers.

The figures in the manuscript are generated by `../figures/make_plots.py` and
`../figures/diagrams/make_diagrams.sh`.
